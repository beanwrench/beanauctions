[SIZE=6][B]BeanAuctions[/B][/SIZE]
[B]Salvage auctions, a diagnostic scan tool and an in-game tablet for career mode.[/B]

Buy wrecked, worn-out and repossessed cars at auction, find out what's actually wrong with them, fix them up and sell them for a profit.

[SIZE=5][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Live auctions.[/B] 100 cars for sale at any time, from every make and model in the game. A new lot goes live about every 40 seconds.
[*][B]Hidden problems.[/B] Sellers don't disclose everything. Some cars have a bad engine, a slipping transmission or an ECU fault you won't see until you inspect them at the yard.
[*][B]Four kinds of sellers.[/B] Insurance write-offs, bank repos, salvage pool wrecks and dealer trade-ins.
[*][B]Sell your own cars[/B] at the salvage sale, or at the Collector Auction for valuable cars and classics. Set a reserve if you like.
[*][B]Restoration value.[/B] A car's value follows the parts actually on it, and fully restored classics can sell for up to twice their price new.
[*][B]Bean Diag.[/B] Read real OBD-II trouble codes from any car you own, with a repair price for each fault.
[*][B]Refurbishing.[/B] Rebuild a car's engine, transmission, driveline, suspension or body with refurbished parts, back to 95% condition. Never brand new.
[*][B]Chop shop.[/B] Strip every removable part off a car in one click from Part Customization.
[*][B]Second monitor.[/B] Open the auction site in your normal web browser while you play.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]How to use[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Load a [B]career[/B] save.
[*]Press [B]B[/B] anywhere, or use the computer in any garage, to open the tablet. [B]Esc[/B] puts it away.
[*]Open [B]BeanAuctions[/B] to buy and sell, or [B]Bean Diag[/B] to scan and refurbish your cars.
[/LIST]

[B]Buying:[/B] set a max bid on any lot and it bids for you, one increment at a time. When the timer runs out the lot goes to a live auction. Inspect expensive cars first, and add up bid + buyer fee + towing + repairs before you commit.

[B]Getting your car:[/B] pick it up at the yard for free, have it towed ($400, about 5 minutes) or pay $1,200 for instant delivery.

[B]Buyer fees:[/B] 12% + $100 under $5,000; 9% + $100 from $5,000 to $19,999; 7% + $100 from $20,000.

[SIZE=5][B]Good to know[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Career mode only. Tested on 0.40.5, West Coast USA. Other career maps with parking spots should work.
[*]B is also the default nitrous key. Rebind either under Options > Controls ("BeanAuctions: open/close tablet").
[*]The value changes apply everywhere the game values your cars: selling, trade-ins, insurance and the auctions.
[*]Browser access only works on your own PC (localhost). The link from the tablet carries a private key.
[*]Suspension and shock wear isn't simulated yet.
[/LIST]
